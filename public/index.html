How FastSCAN works

  1. 1
    Scan a document

    Line the page up in the frame and tap the shutter. FastScan flattens it and makes the text easy to read.

  2. 2
    Add more pages

    Tap + to scan the next page. Keep going until you have them all.

  3. 3
    Make the PDF

    Tap Review to check every page and pick its look, then tap Confirm & make PDF.

  4. 4
    Send it to yourself

    Tap Send to myself and pick your email or chat app to forward the PDF.
